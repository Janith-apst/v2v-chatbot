# Example conversations

Two sample interactions to give you a feel for what the assistant can do. You don't have to follow them step-by-step — ask anything in any order.

## Scenario 1 — General chat and accessibility lookup (no location needed)

Things you can ask:

- "Hello, how are you?"
- "What can you help me with?"
- "What venues do you have in Fitzroy?" (or Melbourne, St Kilda, Footscray, South Yarra, etc.)
- "Tell me about accessibility at Abbotsford Convent."
- "What's at the Shrine of Remembrance?"
- "Say that again."

## Scenario 2 — Near-me flow (uses location)

Things you can ask:

- "What's the nearest accessible venue to me?"
- "What's the closest place in Port Phillip?"
- "Is there anything accessible near me with step-free entry?"
- "Tell me about the nearest one."

If you denied location permission, the assistant will ask which suburb you're near. Tell it e.g. "I'm in St Kilda" and it will list venues from that area.

> Note: location is not always reliable in this POC. On desktops without GPS the browser falls back to IP-based geolocation, which can be off by tens of kilometres or fail entirely. If the answers feel wrong, fall back to scenario 1 and name a suburb directly.
